Everything you need to know about joining and participating in Code Club SCE Saharsa.

All currently enrolled B.Tech students across all departments (Computer Science & Engineering, Mechanical, Civil, Electrical, and Electronics & Communication) and all academic years (1st to 4th year) at Saharsa College of Engineering are eligible to join.
No. Membership in Code Club is 100% free for all students of SCE Saharsa. All our regular workshops, weekly coding contests, and curated learning resource sheets are provided completely free of charge.
